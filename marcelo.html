Marcelo Fernandes

Classical guitarist, composer and professor of music at the Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS). He studied for three years in Uruguay with Abel Carlevaro.

He holds a doctorate in music (2011), a master’s in arts (2003) and a bachelor’s in guitar (1999), all from the University of São Paulo. His doctoral research was on modernism in the guitar music of Camargo Guarnieri, music he also plays in concert.

On SESC’s Sonora Brasil tour he gave 86 guitar recitals in 20 Brazilian states. Abroad he has played in France, Spain, Switzerland, Germany, Belgium, Portugal, Paraguay, Colombia, Bolivia and the United States.

He writes much of what the duo plays: songs on poems by Delasnieve Daspet, Raquel Naveira, Rubênio Marcelo and Carlos Nejar. He also wrote the UFMS anthem and founded the Camerata Madeiras Dedilhadas, the university’s plucked-string ensemble.
