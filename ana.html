Ana Lúcia Gaborim

Soprano, choral conductor and professor at the Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS). She has studied singing with the soprano Adelia Issa for many years.

She holds a doctorate in music from the University of São Paulo (2015), and a master’s in music (2005) and a bachelor’s in composition and conducting (2000) from São Paulo State University (UNESP). Since 2006 she has taught conducting, choral singing, vocal physiology and vocal technique on the UFMS music degree.

She coordinates three choral outreach projects: CanteMus, a voice lab; PCIU!, the UFMS children’s and youth choir; and Paraná Canta, which takes choirs into public schools with the Paraná Department of Education and four public universities. From 2021 to 2024 she was president of ABRACO, the Brazilian Association of Choral Conductors.
