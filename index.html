The duo

Married since 1998, on stage together since 2015.

Duo Amábile performs Brazilian art song for voice and guitar, from nineteenth-century modinhas to works written for the duo. Much of the repertoire is by Marcelo Fernandes, who sets poems by Brazilian writers such as Carlos Nejar, Raquel Naveira and Rubênio Marcelo, and Ana Lúcia Gaborim gives the first performance.

The duo made its debut in 2015 in SESC’s Partituras series, in Corumbá. It has since performed in six countries, at venues including the Brazilian Academy of Letters, the Université Sorbonne Nouvelle, the University of Aveiro and the residence of the Brazilian Ambassador in Bern, and has toured sixteen towns in Mato Grosso do Sul.

Concerts

Selected concerts and projects since 2015.

  1. 2015

    SESC Partituras, the first concert

    SESC theatre · Corumbá, MS

    Invited to the SESC Partituras project, they played Brazilian chamber music written or arranged for voice and guitar, published by SESC.

    Concerts followed at the UFMS campuses in Chapadão do Sul, Coxim, Paranaíba, Ponta Porã and Três Lagoas. At the end of the year the duo closed FETEC at the Memorial da Cultura de MS.

  2. 2016

    First studio recording

    Campo Grande and other towns in MS

    The duo recorded “As árvores do meu lugar”, a poem by Eliane Guaraldo set by Marcelo Fernandes, for the CD “As canções que fizemos para ela: homenagem a Maria Betânia”, an independent release by teachers and composers from UFMS.

    They also played at academic and cultural events that year, including the Chá Literário of the Academia Sul-mato-grossense de Letras at the Centro Cultural José Otávio Guizzo.

  3. 2017

    A música brasileira em seis cordas

    Eight towns in MS

    A tribute to the guitarist Dilermando Reis, funded by the state culture fund (FIC). From May to September the duo played in Bonito, Corguinho, Naviraí, Nova Andradina, Ponta Porã, Rochedo, São Gabriel do Oeste and Sidrolândia.

    The final concert, at Teatro Glauce Rocha in Campo Grande, had guest artists Eduardo Martinelli, Luciana Fisher and Renato Oliveira. In October the duo was back at the same theatre for Movimento Concerto.

  4. 2019

    Canções poéticas e imagens modernistas

    Around Campo Grande · FMIC

    With a grant from the city culture fund (FMIC), the project produced a series of videos filmed at landmarks around Campo Grande, with violinist Heitor Lotti and soprano Andressa Chinzarian.

    In June, “Fogo da Poesia” was premiered at the Academia Brasileira de Letras de Mato Grosso do Sul and broadcast on TV Pantanal.

  5. 20202021

    Concerts with no audience

    Teatro Glauce Rocha · live stream

    During the pandemic the duo kept playing in the online edition of Movimento Concerto: concerts at Teatro Glauce Rocha with empty seats, streamed live by TV UFMS. Two of them are on the videos page.

  6. 2021

    “Ode à Campo Grande” becomes a music video

    Meu Mato Grosso do Sul · TV Morena

    Marcelo’s setting of a poem by Rubênio Marcelo was chosen for the city’s anniversary. The duo recorded it in the studio and it was made into a music video.

    Around this time Marcelo wrote a set of songs for the duo on poems by Delasnieve Daspet, Rubênio Marcelo and Carlos Nejar, performed at the Academia Sul-mato-grossense de Letras and at AFLAMS events. The duo also played live on “Tudo de Bom”, on SBT.

    Watch the video on Globoplay

  7. 2022

    Encontro com a Música Clássica

    Teatro Glauce Rocha · August

    The duo played at the festival run by the Campo Grande Municipal Symphony Orchestra.

    Between April and July they had played at the Instituto Histórico e Geográfico de MS, recorded for TV Assembleia, paid tribute to the poet Raquel Naveira and played at the launch of the magazine “Estação Folclore” at SESC Cultura. At the end of the year the FIC fund backed a new project, “Cem anos de Semana de Arte Moderna em Terras Pantaneiras”.

  8. 2023

    Brazilian Academy of Letters, Rio

    ABL and PEN Clube do Brasil · Rio de Janeiro

    The duo played at PEN Clube do Brasil for the founding of PEN Clube Centro-Oeste, and at the Academia Brasileira de Letras, where they premiered a piece by Marcelo Fernandes on a poem by Carlos Nejar.

    In April they had opened the International Guitar Festival at Teatro Glauce Rocha.

  9. 2023

    Cem anos de Semana de Arte Moderna em Terras Pantaneiras

    Six towns in MS

    The FIC-funded project took the duo to Aquidauana, Corumbá, Coxim, Dourados, Ponta Porã and Três Lagoas, in partnership with UEMS, IFMS and UFMS. Every concert was free.

    The closing concert, on 11 October, celebrated the anniversary of the state of Mato Grosso do Sul at Bioparque Pantanal, to a full house.

  10. 2023

    Memorial da América Latina

    São Paulo · September

    The duo opened the 8th IFLAC World Brazil Peace Congress, with violinist Heitor Lotti as guest.

    Also in 2023, the duo recorded two songs by Marcelo on poems by Raquel Naveira, “Guaicurus” and “O Andarilho das Américas”, for the CD “Cabeza de Vaca, Andarilho das Américas”, produced by Moacir Lacerda and launched at Teatro Glauce Rocha.

    Listen to “Guaicurus” on Spotify Listen to “O Andarilho das Américas” on Spotify

  11. 2024

    ALMAR project: fado

    SESC Prosa · 8 October

    A concert of Portuguese fado with the poets Rubênio Marcelo and Ana Maria Bernardelli and guest musicians: soprano Luciana Fisher, Luiz Sayd and Joel Mendes.

  12. 2024

    Aveiro, Festivais de Outono

    Igreja das Carmelitas · Portugal · November

    Invited by the University of Aveiro’s autumn festival. The programme had European and Brazilian songs, including pieces by Marcelo Fernandes.

  13. 2024

    Nepomuk, Svatojánské Muzeum

    Czech Republic · November

    A concert in the museum’s cultural programme, at the invitation of the Czech consulate, on the same trip that took the duo to Portugal.

  14. 2025

    Bern and Paris

    Switzerland and France · March and April

    In Bern they played at the residence of the Brazilian Ambassador, for ambassadors from several countries. In Paris, at the Maison de l’Amérique Latine and the Université Sorbonne Nouvelle.

    The programme had Marcelo’s songs on poems by Delasnieve Daspet, Raquel Naveira and Rubênio Marcelo, and works by the Brazilian composer Otto Pintiaski. On the same tour Marcelo gave a solo recital at the Winterthur Conservatory.

  15. 2025

    Umbria: Todi and Deruta

    Italy · July

    Two concerts in Umbria: in Todi, at the festival Suoni dal legno, and at the Museo Regionale della Ceramica in Deruta.

  16. 2025

    Ceremonies and openings

    Campo Grande, MS

    The swearing-in of the OAB (Brazilian Bar Association) board at Teatro Rubens Gil de Camillo on 29 January; welcome week for UFMS music students at the Anfiteatro Luís Felipe de Oliveira in March; and the opening of COP 30 Pantanal Biome at the Insted auditorium on 30 September.

  17. 2026

    Femina Vox and the city’s schools

    Teatro Rubens Gil de Camilo · 16 March

    The duo opened Femina Vox, an international event held in Campo Grande.

    Between August and September, the project “Entre o rural e o urbano: as canções de Almir Sater e Chico Buarque” took the duo to municipal schools in rural areas and outlying neighbourhoods. The project was covered by the TV news programme “Bom Dia MS”.

Repertoire

The programme changes with the occasion. At its centre is Brazilian song, above all contemporary and regional song.

  1. Early music

    16th to 18th century

    European pieces for voice and plucked instrument.

  2. Classical and Romantic

    19th century

    Lieder and salon songs, arranged for voice and guitar.

  3. Twentieth-century song

    20th century

    Works written or arranged for voice and guitar.

  4. Contemporary Brazilian song

    Today · the duo’s focus

    New and regional work, much of it by Marcelo Fernandes on Brazilian poems, including songs in Nheengatu, an Indigenous language of the Amazon, first sung on the 2025 European tour.
